To pass a level, complete all target words within 50 seconds. Accuracy determines your star rating:

Rating Requirement
1 Star Complete all words before 50s & Accuracy ≥ 60%
2 Stars Complete all words before 60s & Accuracy ≥ 80%
3 Stars Complete all words & Accuracy ≥ 80% in ≤ 48 seconds
0 Stars Passed with Accuracy < 60%, or time expired
